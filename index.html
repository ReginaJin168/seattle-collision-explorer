Method

Method & interpretation

Data. Cleaned SDOT collision records (30 rows from a different extract removed, swapped coordinates fixed), joined in Python to Open-Meteo hourly weather.

Missing time. Reports with no recorded time are stored as 00:00. They are kept in counts and the map but left out of the hourly chart, so they do not create a false midnight peak.

Missingness. Weather, road, and lighting fields are unavailable for some records and are preserved as “Unknown” rather than silently removed.

Interpretation. Collision counts do not measure underlying risk without exposure denominators. Observed relationships should not be interpreted as causal effects.
